# 👁️ Computer Vision — How Machines See

> **Teaching machines to understand the visual world.**

Computer Vision is a field of AI that enables machines to process and interpret images and videos.

Humans see an image and instantly recognize objects, faces, colors, and patterns. Computer vision attempts to achieve similar capabilities using **data, mathematics, and deep learning**.

---

## 🔍 How Computer Vision Works

A simplified pipeline looks like this:

```text
Image
  ↓
Pixels
  ↓
Preprocessing
  ↓
Feature Extraction
  ↓
Deep Learning Model
  ↓
Prediction
```

An image is first represented as numerical pixel values. The model then learns patterns within those numbers.

---

## 🧠 CNNs — Convolutional Neural Networks

CNNs became one of the most important architectures for image understanding.

They learn visual features progressively:

```text
Pixels
  ↓
Edges
  ↓
Textures
  ↓
Shapes
  ↓
Objects
  ↓
Classification
```

A simple CNN can be represented as:

```text
Image
 ↓
Convolution
 ↓
Activation
 ↓
Pooling
 ↓
Convolution
 ↓
Fully Connected Layer
 ↓
Output
```

* 🙂 Face recognition

* 🛰️ Satellite image analysis
* 🔎 Image search

---

## 🌍 Beyond CNNs

Modern computer vision is no longer limited to CNNs.

Today's systems also use:

```text
CNNs
 ↓
Vision Transformers
 ↓
Multimodal Models
 ↓
Vision + Language
```

This allows AI systems to connect **what they see** with **what they understand**.

---

## 🏁 Final Thought

> **Computer vision isn't about teaching machines to see like humans.**
>
> **It's about teaching machines to extract meaning from pixels.**

From a collection of numbers to understanding an entire scene — **computer vision turns pixels into perception.** 👁️🤖
